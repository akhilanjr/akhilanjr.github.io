I'm a Senior Research Associate at [J-PAL South Asia](https://www.povertyactionlab.org/person/j-r), based in Chennai. I work on large-scale randomized controlled trials, government partnerships and education pilots across Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra, alongside Principal Investigators from MIT and J-PAL.

[View my CV]({{ "/cv/" | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn--primary}

## Current work

**Identifying and Nurturing Math Talent at Scale.** I lead research operations and policy coordination for this study with Esther Duflo, Glenn Ellison, Sara Ellison, Phi Adajar and Harini Kannan. It evaluates self-paced Art of Problem Solving (AoPS) online courses and tutoring for 2,492+ government school students across 10 districts of Tamil Nadu, with expansions to Grades 4–6 and 11–12 in Tamil Nadu and a Grades 7–10 pilot in Maharashtra with MSCERT.

## Research interests

Economics of education, experimental methods, EdTech and AI for development, state capacity, and administrative data systems.

I'm currently developing a doctoral proposal, *Supporting First Generation Learners in Tamil Nadu's Primary Schools*, which uses longitudinal administrative data and proposes an RCT of community tutoring and multilingual voice-based AI tutors to bridge home-input gaps.
